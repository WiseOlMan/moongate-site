Privacy Policy

ColorGuess: Mind Reader · Last updated: 25 October 2026

ColorGuess: Mind Reader does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

Microphone and speech recognition

The app asks for microphone and speech recognition access so it can hear the color you say. Speech is processed on your device using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your audio and transcripts are never recorded, saved, or sent to me or to any server.

What stays on your device

Your score for the current session exists only in memory while you play. The app stores two small preferences on your device, whether you have seen the introduction and whether countdown sounds are on. Neither leaves your device.
