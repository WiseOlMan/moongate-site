ColorGuess: Mind Reader

Close your eyes. Say the color you sense.

ColorGuess hides a color, rings a chime, and listens while you say the color you sense. Then it tells you the real color, says it out loud, and keeps your score, so you can see how well your intuition holds up.

  1. Tap Start. A 5-second countdown gives you time to close your eyes.
  2. When the chime rings, say the color you sense.
  3. ColorGuess listens, checks your answer, and says the real color.
  4. Keep going. Every new color comes with a chime, and your accuracy updates as you play.
  • Private by design: speech is recognized on your device. No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no data collected.
  • Made to feel good: haptics, smooth animations, Reduce Motion support, and high-contrast text on every color.
