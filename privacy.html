Privacy Policy

Moon Gate · Last updated: 22 August 2026

Moon Gate (Mac menu extra and independent Apple Watch app) does not create accounts, does not track you, and does not sell data.

The apps request the current Human Design moon gate from https://human-design-ai-chatbot.vercel.app/api/public/moon, including your device time zone so the transit matches local time. That request is not linked to your name, Apple ID, or a user identifier we store.

No analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.
