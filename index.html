Moon Gate: Human Design

The Moon's current gate, always one glance away.

Moon Gate shows the Human Design gate the Moon is moving through right now: the hexagram, gate number, line, center, and how much time remains. It lives in your Mac menu bar and on your Apple Watch face, so you can stay in tune with the transit without opening a chart.

  • Mac menu bar widget: current gate, line, and center at a glance. Open it for the reading, keywords, and what's coming next.
  • Apple Watch app and complication: a standalone watchOS app with a watch face complication.
  • Private by design: no accounts, no tracking, no analytics.
